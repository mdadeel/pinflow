# Privacy Policy for PinFlow by WallPeps

**Effective Date:** August 27, 2026

## 1. Overview
PinFlow ("we", "our", or "us") is a Pinterest automation platform developed by WallPeps. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our PinFlow platform.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- Pinterest account credentials (via OAuth access token)
- Pinterest board information (board names, descriptions)
- Pin content (images, titles, descriptions, keywords)
- Scheduling preferences and automation settings

### 2.2 Information We Automatically Collect
- Platform usage statistics
- API request logs (for debugging and optimization)
- Error reports and performance metrics

### 2.3 Information from Pinterest
When you connect your Pinterest account, we may collect:
- Public profile information
- Board information
- Pin analytics (impressions, clicks, saves)
- Account preferences and settings

## 3. How We Use Your Information
- To provide and maintain PinFlow services
- To schedule and automate Pinterest pin creation
- To analyze Pinterest performance metrics
- To improve platform features and user experience
- To communicate about service updates or issues
- To comply with legal obligations

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share information:
- With service providers who assist in platform operation
- To comply with legal requirements or protect rights
- During business transfers (mergers, acquisitions)
- With your consent

## 5. Pinterest Data Usage
PinFlow accesses your Pinterest data through Pinterest's official API. We:
- Only access data needed for pin automation
- Never access private or sensitive information beyond what's required
- Comply with Pinterest's Developer Terms and Acceptable Use Policy
- Store data securely and for only as long as needed

## 6. Data Security
We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures:
- Encryption of data in transit and at rest
- Secure authentication via OAuth
- Regular security audits
- Limited access to authorized personnel only

## 7. Data Retention
We retain data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us.

## 8. Your Rights
Depending on your location, you may have rights to:
- Access your personal information
- Correct inaccurate information
- Delete your information
- Restrict or object to processing
- Data portability

## 9. Third-Party Links
Our platform may contain links to third-party services (Pinterest, image providers). We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

## 10. Children's Privacy
PinFlow is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## 11. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify you of changes by posting the new policy on this page.
